Who We Are

Our Story

Two Entities, One Mission

Kenshi.AI is a Rwanda-based technology company specializing in software development, machine learning, AI engineering, and data science to help organizations transform data into real value.

Founded in Kigali and aligned with Rwanda's Vision 2050, we operate at the intersection of innovation, research, and real-world impact.

Our AI and Digital Innovation Hub bridges education and industry by connecting learning with real projects, building sustainable AI capacity through practical training and data-driven solutions across Africa.

Legal Form

Private Limited Company (Ltd.), registered with Rwanda Development Board (RDB).

Governance

Founder and CEO, supported by a Board of Advisors for strategic guidance.

Headquarters

Kigali, Rwanda, serving organizations across Africa.

The Challenge We Address

  • Many learners gain theory but lack hands-on project experience
  • Organizations have data but cannot convert it into decisions
  • Promising ideas fail because technical support is missing
  • Imported AI solutions can be expensive and hard to sustain locally

Result: Skills gaps, slower innovation, and missed opportunities.

Our Approach

  • Train talent inside a real AI delivery environment
  • Help organizations make better decisions with data
  • Support innovators from concept to working products
  • Build local capacity rather than long-term dependency

Note: This is not just training - it is applied innovation.
