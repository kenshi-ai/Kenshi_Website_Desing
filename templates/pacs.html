How RadAIx Works

A 5-step workflow from scan upload to real-time clinical alerts.

01

CT Scan Upload

Medical images are uploaded to RadAIx through standard DICOM workflows - no disruption for hospital staff.

02

AI Analysis

Deep learning analyzes each slice and detects hemorrhage subtypes.

Intraparenchymal (IPH) Subarachnoid (SAH) Subdural (SDH) Epidural (EDH) Intraventricular (IVH)
03

Visual Intelligence

Generates heatmaps and confidence scores (0-100%) to explain what the model is seeing.

04

Priority Flagging

Suspected hemorrhages move to the top of the worklist with urgency indicators.

05

Clinical Alert

Real-time notifications can reach emergency teams so treatment can start sooner.
